Alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goal 3

Good Health & Well-Being • Target 3.4: Reduce Mortality from Non-Communicable Diseases

Brain tumors are among the most lethal oncological conditions globally. Timely detection, accurate tumor differentiation, and early surgical triage significantly improve patient survival rates. In low- and middle-income regions, neuro-radiologists are critically scarce. NeuroVision AI addresses this health disparity by democratizing access to expert-level diagnostic triage, reducing MRI analysis latency from hours to under 150 milliseconds.

Why EfficientNetB0?

Traditional convolutional networks (such as ResNet-50 or VGG-16) arbitrarily scale network depth or width, leading to diminishing returns and excessive computational cost (over 25M to 138M parameters).

EfficientNetB0 utilizes a principled compound scaling method that uniformly scales depth ($\alpha$), width ($\beta$), and image resolution ($\gamma$) using a compound coefficient ($\phi$):

depth: d = αφ • width: w = βφ • resolution: r = γφ
s.t. α • β2 • γ2 ≈ 2

This achieves state-of-the-art accuracy with only 5.3 million parameters, enabling real-time edge deployment and near-zero latency inference inside hospital environments.

Two-Stage Fine-Tuning Pipeline
Stage 1 Transfer Learning Feature Extraction (10 Epochs)

The EfficientNetB0 backbone pre-trained on ImageNet is frozen. Only the customized classification head (GlobalAveragePooling2D, BatchNormalization, Dense(256), Dropout(0.4), Softmax) is trained with an Adam learning rate of 1e-3.

Stage 2 Targeted Backbone Fine-Tuning (15 Epochs)

The top 30 layers of the EfficientNetB0 backbone are unfrozen. A reduced learning rate of 1e-4 is applied, while BatchNormalization layers remain frozen in inference mode to safeguard running statistical stability.

Explainable AI: Mathematical Formulation of Grad-CAM

In high-stakes medical diagnosis, "black-box" models cannot be deployed without interpretability. NeuroVision AI implements Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) to visually prove that the neural network focuses on true neoplastic tissue rather than background MRI artifacts.

1. Neuron Importance Weights (αkc):

αkc = (1 / Z) ∑i ∑j (∂ yc / ∂ Ai,jk)

Global average pooling of gradients of the predicted class score yc with respect to feature map activations Ak at the top_conv layer.
2. Heatmap Localization Map (LGrad-CAMc):

LGrad-CAMc = ReLU( ∑k αkc Ak )

Linear combination of activation maps followed by a Rectified Linear Unit (ReLU) to isolate features that positively contribute to the target tumor class.
