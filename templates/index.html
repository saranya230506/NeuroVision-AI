Detectable Brain MRI Pathologies

Glioma Intra-axial Glial Tumors

Arises from brain astrocytes or oligodendrocytes. Can range from low-grade to glioblastoma multiforme. Often presents with focal seizures, nausea, and morning headaches.

Meningioma Extra-axial Dural Tumors

Originates from the arachnoid cap cells of the meninges. Typically well-circumscribed, benign lesions that compress neural parenchyma without direct infiltration.

Pituitary Adenoma Sella Turcica Endocrine Tumors

Forms in the pituitary gland at the base of the brain. Often triggers neuroendocrine imbalances and optic chiasm compression causing bitemporal hemianopsia.

No Tumor Normal Brain Parenchyma

Clear cerebral hemispheres, symmetric ventricular dimensions, preserved gray-white differentiation, and midline stability with zero space-occupying masses.
